Support

Get help with a FastKiwi app

Found a problem, or have an idea? Tell us, in English or Arabic. You don’t need a GitHub account.

Many answers are already in the FAQ and in each app’s release notes.

Contact us without GitHub

Email

Write to us in English or Arabic. A person reads every message.

support@fastkiwi.app

Email us

What to include

  1. The app: InstaFind, Remember Me, Lemon or InstaWords (and for InstaWords, whether it’s the Windows app or the web app).
  2. The app version: shown in the app’s About or Settings.
  3. Your Windows version: Windows 10 or 11, and the version shown in Settings, System, About (or type winver in Start).
  4. The steps: what you did, step by step, what you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot of the problem helps a lot.

Never include

Amounts you converted, your tasks, notes or chats, file names or file contents, passwords, account or card numbers, or any other personal or financial information. Cover them in screenshots too. We never need them to fix a problem.

Tip: InstaWords’ “Report a problem” window and Remember Me’s “Copy diagnostic info” collect the app and Windows versions for you, without your data, and you can copy them into your message.

Common questions

Windows says “Unknown publisher” or “Windows protected your PC”. Is it safe?

The files are not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen doesn’t recognise the publisher. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. Every release is built automatically, and the official downloads page shows how to check that your file is genuine with its SHA-256 fingerprint.

My antivirus flagged the download. What should I do?

New programs that aren’t code-signed are sometimes flagged by mistake (a “false positive”), especially small single-file apps. Download only from this site, check the file with a second scanner such as VirusTotal if you like, and tell us so we can report it to the antivirus maker.

Do the apps really work without the internet?

Yes. Everything runs on your device. The internet is used only to check for updates, to download Lemon’s engine and AI models (from github.com and huggingface.co), for Lemon’s optional Online mode, and for InstaFind’s exchange rates. The privacy page lists every connection.

How do I uninstall an app?

Apps that come with an installer, like Remember Me and InstaWords, are removed from Windows Settings › Apps › Installed apps, like any other program. Single-file apps such as InstaFind and Lemon don’t install anything: close the app and delete the .exe file. On Android, remove Scan Kiwi or Remember Me like any other app.

Are the apps really free?

Yes. They are free, with no ads or subscriptions.

Which Windows versions are supported?

Windows 10 and 11. InstaFind also runs on ARM64 and 32-bit PCs, and InstaWords also works in any modern browser.

How do I report a problem or suggest a feature?

Email us (see Contact us above), or open an issue on the app’s GitHub page if you have an account (see Open a GitHub issue). Say what to include from the list above. Reports and ideas are welcome in English or Arabic.

Your privacy

We only see what you choose to send us. No app sends reports, crash data or usage statistics on its own. The privacy policy lists what each app stores and every network connection it makes.
