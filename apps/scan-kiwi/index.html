What it does

Scanning

  • Finds the page and captures it by itself
  • Easy manual crop with a magnifier
  • Fixes the angle and removes shadows

The document

  • Many pages: reorder, rotate and delete
  • Import photos and PDFs
  • A signature, and both sides of an ID card

Export

  • Small PDFs for WhatsApp
  • Searchable Arabic and English text, offline
  • A password for the file, and JPG images

What’s new in 2.0.3?

  • A build for F-Droid and IzzyOnDroid with no internet permission: the camera is its only permission
  • Scanning and export are unchanged, and all earlier fixes are included
