What it does

Alerts that don’t get lost

  • A sticky notification with Done, Snooze and Undo
  • A full-screen alarm for the important ones
  • Up to 3 early alerts

Quick to add

  • Type “meeting tomorrow at 5” and it understands the time
  • Templates, voice input and sharing from other apps
  • Times based on prayer times and the Hijri calendar

Organised and reliable

  • Lists, tags and day, week and month views
  • “Reminder health” checks what could delay an alert
  • Encrypted backups, and the same file in Remember Me for Windows

What’s new in 1.0.6?

  • Installs over 1.0.5 and keeps your reminders
