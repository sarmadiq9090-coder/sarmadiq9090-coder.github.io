Remember Me™ · reminders for Android

Reminders that stay until you deal with them

Remember Me for Android keeps each reminder on your phone until you mark it done or snooze it. No account and no ads, Arabic first with English, and open source.

Download the APK from GitHub How to install

Free · Android 8.0 or later · Arabic and English · not on Google Play

Features

Made so a reminder is not missed

Your reminders are stored on your phone, in the same data file as Remember Me for Windows.

Alerts that stay

A pinned notification with Done, Snooze 10 minutes and Snooze until…, and a full-screen alert for important reminders. An important alarm rings at alarm volume, even in silent mode.

Quick Add

Type “meeting tomorrow at 5” in Arabic or English and the date and time are understood, with a preview before anything is saved. Templates, voice input and a Quick Settings tile too.

Flexible repeats

Daily, weekly, chosen days, every few days, monthly with month ends, yearly, Hijri dates and times relative to prayer times.

Lists and calendar

Overdue, Today, Upcoming and Done in one list, with search, lists, tags and steps, and agenda, day, week and month views.

Reminder health

Checks what on your phone could delay an alert, such as battery limits or exact alarms, and offers one fix per problem. “Test the alert now” shows it works.

Backups you control

Encrypted backups, a weekly automatic copy to a folder you choose, calendar import and export, and backups that move to and from Remember Me for Windows.

Privacy

Your reminders stay on your phone

  • Internet only for updates. Remember Me connects to the internet only to check for and download updates from GitHub. It sends no reminder and no data about you.
  • No account, ads or trackers. Nothing is collected about how you use the app.
  • Arabic first, English included. Full right-to-left layout, light and dark themes, and TalkBack labels.
  • Open source under Apache-2.0. Anyone can read the code and build it.

Install

Install the APK in four steps

Remember Me for Android is not on Google Play, so you install the file yourself. Android asks you to allow installs from the app you download it with.

Steps

  1. Download the file RememberMe-X.Y.Z.apk from the latest release on GitHub, on your phone.
  2. Open the file from the download notification or your Files app.
  3. Allow the install. Android shows a message about unknown apps: tap Settings, turn on “Allow from this source”, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open, and allow notifications.

What you need

  • Android 8.0 or later.
  • Notifications allowed for the app, so reminders can appear.
  • Using Windows? Remember Me for Windows is a separate app, and it is not open source. The two exchange data through a backup file.

Updates

How to get new versions

In the app, Settings has “Check for updates”, and the app also checks its GitHub releases by itself at most once a week, over Wi-Fi only by default. An update is installed only if it is newer, its SHA-256 matches the release’s SHA256SUMS.txt and it is signed with the app’s own key, and only when you confirm on Android’s screen. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any website.

You can also update by hand: download the latest APK and install it over the old one. Your reminders stay.

FAQ

Questions about Remember Me for Android

More answers in the FastKiwi FAQ.

Does Remember Me for Android use the internet?

Only to check for and download updates from GitHub, and you can check by hand instead. Reminders, alerts and backups all work on your phone without the internet. Voice input uses your phone’s own speech service, which may use the internet on its side.

Why isn’t it on Google Play?

Remember Me for Android is published only in its GitHub releases. That is why Android asks you to allow installs from this source the first time.

Android warns me about installing unknown apps. What should I do?

This message appears for every app that doesn’t come from an app store. Download the APK only from our GitHub releases, and you can compare its SHA-256 fingerprint with the release’s SHA256SUMS.txt.

What if a reminder arrives late?

Some phones limit apps in the background to save battery. Open “Reminder health” in the app: it shows what your phone limits and how to fix it, and “Test the alert now” checks the result.

Is this the same as Remember Me for Windows?

No. Remember Me for Windows is a separate app and is not open source. The Android app reads and writes the same backup format, so you can move your reminders between them.

Can I see the code?

Yes. Remember Me for Android is open source under the Apache-2.0 licence, with build instructions in its GitHub repository. The name and icon are trademarks and are not covered by the licence.
